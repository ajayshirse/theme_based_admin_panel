About cosmetic dentistry

A cosmetic plan begins with listening. We look at your smile in photographs and in person, then show you the options before any work begins.

Treatments range from a single chipped tooth repaired with bonding to a full set of porcelain veneers. Every plan balances appearance with long-term tooth health.

Typical treatment: 1–3 visits

Benefits

  • Natural-looking shape, shade and proportion
  • Minimally invasive options wherever possible
  • Preview of the planned result before treatment
  • Care instructions to keep results looking fresh

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Smile consultation

    We talk through what you would like to change and take photographs and scans.

  2. Step 2. Treatment plan

    You receive a clear written plan with options, timings and costs.

  3. Step 3. Treatment visits

    Most cosmetic work takes one to three visits.

  4. Step 4. Review

    We check comfort and appearance and book your maintenance visits.

About teeth whitening

Professional whitening uses controlled-strength gels and custom trays or in-chair lights, so the shade lifts evenly while your gums are protected.

We check for decay and gum problems first, because whitening works best on healthy teeth.

Typical treatment: 60–90 min

Benefits

  • Noticeably lighter shade in a single session
  • Gum protection during treatment
  • Take-home trays for top-ups
  • Advice for reducing sensitivity

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Health check

    We examine teeth and gums and record your starting shade.

  2. Step 2. Protection

    Gums and lips are shielded before gel is applied.

  3. Step 3. Whitening

    In-chair sessions take about an hour; home kits run for one to two weeks.

  4. Step 4. Aftercare

    We explain which foods and drinks to limit for the first days.

About dental implants

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jaw that supports a crown, bridge or denture. It looks and feels like a natural tooth and helps preserve the bone around it.

Implant planning uses 3D scans so the post is placed precisely and comfortably. Healing time varies, and we explain each stage so there are no surprises.

Typical treatment: 3–4 visits over several months

Benefits

  • Fixed, stable replacement teeth
  • Helps maintain jawbone volume
  • No need to alter neighbouring teeth
  • Designed to last with good care

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Assessment and scan

    A 3D scan checks bone volume and the best position for the implant.

  2. Step 2. Placement

    The post is placed under local anaesthetic. Sedation options are available.

  3. Step 3. Healing

    The bone bonds with the implant over a period your dentist will explain.

  4. Step 4. Final crown

    A custom crown is fitted and the bite is fine-tuned.

About orthodontics

Straight teeth are easier to clean and often more comfortable to chew with. We offer traditional braces, ceramic braces and removable clear aligners for teens and adults.

Your orthodontist will explain how long treatment is likely to take and what is needed from you between visits.

Typical treatment: Adjustments every 4–8 weeks

Benefits

  • Options for teens and adults
  • Clear aligners for a discreet look
  • Regular progress checks
  • Retainers to keep results stable

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Records and scans

    Photographs, X-rays and scans show how your teeth and jaws fit together.

  2. Step 2. Plan

    You see the proposed tooth movements and the appliance options.

  3. Step 3. Active treatment

    Short adjustment visits keep treatment on track.

  4. Step 4. Retention

    Retainers hold your new smile in place.

About root canal treatment

When the inside of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, a root canal removes the damaged tissue, cleans the space and seals it. The aim is to relieve pain and keep your own tooth.

Modern anaesthetics and magnification make the procedure comfortable. Most people describe it as similar to having a filling placed.

Typical treatment: 1–2 visits

Benefits

  • Relief from tooth pain and swelling
  • Keeps your natural tooth
  • Performed under local anaesthetic
  • Crown protection afterwards

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Diagnosis

    An X-ray and tests confirm which tooth needs treatment.

  2. Step 2. Cleaning

    The canals are cleaned and shaped under local anaesthetic.

  3. Step 3. Sealing

    The space is filled and sealed to prevent reinfection.

  4. Step 4. Crown

    A crown or filling restores strength and appearance.

About gum treatment

Bleeding, swollen or receding gums are signs your gums need attention. Early gum disease is reversible, and treating it protects the bone that supports your teeth.

Treatment usually combines a thorough professional clean with coaching on home care, and follow-up visits to check healing.

Typical treatment: 1–3 visits

Benefits

  • Reduces bleeding and inflammation
  • Protects the bone around your teeth
  • Fresher breath
  • Personal home-care coaching

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Gum assessment

    We measure the depth of the spaces around each tooth.

  2. Step 2. Deep clean

    Plaque and tartar are removed above and below the gumline.

  3. Step 3. Home-care plan

    We tailor brushing and cleaning advice to your mouth.

  4. Step 4. Review

    We re-check healing and set your maintenance schedule.

About dental fillings

Fillings repair teeth damaged by decay or small fractures. We use tooth-coloured composite material that bonds to the tooth and is shaped to match your bite.

Catching cavities early keeps fillings small, so regular check-ups and X-rays matter.

Typical treatment: 30–60 min

Benefits

  • Matches the colour of your teeth
  • Conserves healthy tooth structure
  • Usually completed in one visit
  • Numbing options for a comfortable visit

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Examination

    We locate and assess the cavity.

  2. Step 2. Numbing

    Local anaesthetic keeps you comfortable.

  3. Step 3. Repair

    Decay is removed and the filling is placed in layers.

  4. Step 4. Bite check

    We polish the filling and check how your teeth meet.

About kids dentistry

Good dental habits start early. Our children's visits are short, friendly and explained in plain words, so a trip to the dentist feels ordinary rather than scary.

We cover check-ups, cleaning, fluoride, fissure sealants and advice on thumb-sucking, dummies and snacking.

Typical treatment: 30 min

Benefits

  • Gentle, child-friendly approach
  • Protective sealants for back teeth
  • Advice for parents at every stage
  • Bright children's corner in our waiting area

What to expect

  1. Step 1. Meet and greet

    Your child explores the room and meets the team first.

  2. Step 2. Check-up

    A quick, gentle look at teeth, gums and bite.

  3. Step 3. Clean and protect

    A polish, fluoride and sealants if needed.

  4. Step 4. Parent advice

    We share simple tips for brushing and diet at home.

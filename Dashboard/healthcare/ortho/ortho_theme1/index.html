About Us

An orthodontic clinic built around you

SmileCraft Orthodontics is a specialist clinic dedicated to aligning teeth and jaws for better health, function and confidence. Every plan starts with listening to your goals, then combining careful clinical assessment with the latest technology.

We keep our approach simple: clear explanations, honest timelines and gentle care at every visit, whether you are a child starting early intervention or an adult finally ready to straighten your smile.

Options for every smile and lifestyle

Braces

Metal and ceramic braces remain the most reliable way to correct crowding, gaps and complex bite problems.

Treatment Process

Your journey to a new smile

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We discuss your concerns, goals and medical history in a relaxed first visit.

  2. 2

    Dental Assessment

    X-rays, photographs and digital scans give us a complete picture of your bite.

  3. 3

    Treatment Planning

    We recommend the best option and explain the timeline, steps and costs clearly.

  4. 4

    Follow-up
